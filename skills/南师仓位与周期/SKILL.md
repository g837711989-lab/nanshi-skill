# 南师仓位与周期

## When
User asks about BTC/ETH/alt allocation, when to buy alts, cycle phase, fear/greed vs dominance, rebalancing, or “现在该加还是减”.

## Steps
1. Load if present: alt-pain／仓位 notes (e.g. 规避山寨痛苦), strategy cheat sheet (老奶奶／FGI／VA), cycle report summary (`从历史看懂趋势与周期`), mindfulness notes (`投资的正念`), and 人设负面清单.
2. Ask only what you still need (one at a time): roughly idle-money vs total, current BTC/ETH/alt/stable mix, and whether they mean long-hold or a sleeve strategy. Skip if already given.
3. Diagnose with source frameworks—not price calls:
   - **骨架**：大饼主力（sources often ~70%）、姨太次之、小山寨合计约三成且再细分；约半年再平衡（as in notes）
   - **买山寨时机**：偏熊、BTC 市占高（资料常提 ≥55%，更好 ≥60%）；忌山寨季 FOMO
   - **卖／减小山寨**：市占逼近约 40%、山寨指数约 90、或老奶奶对 BTC 翻倍递推；双指标共振；指标可失灵，逃顶留余地
   - **周期相位**：嵌套周期／识相位非点位；顶部收缩、底部布局；查杠杆、现金流、情绪、是否被迫离场
   - **正念**：觉察／接纳／当下／纪律；震荡少交易
4. Output a short checklist: keep / trim / wait—with which rule from the notes. Flag what the sources do not settle.
5. Never shout 明天涨跌. Prefer “按资料，条件未齐／已齐” language. Non-advice disclaimer.

## Do not
- Recommend leverage or all-in alts.
- Treat single indicators as certainty.
- Invent MVRV sell schedules beyond what the notes state.
